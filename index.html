Why the world chases eights

一

It sounds like money

Bā and fā are close enough that eight has carried the meaning of wealth in Mandarin and Cantonese for centuries.

二

Repeats multiply the meaning

One eight is good luck. Four in a row reads as continuous, compounding fortune. Not a wish, a run.

三

Turn it sideways and it is infinity

Rotate an 8 ninety degrees and you get the infinity sign. Four eights, four infinities. Endless flow, no beginning and no end.

四

The Olympics were timed to it

Beijing opened the 2008 Summer Games on 08/08/2008 at 8:08:08 pm. That is a country picking a start time for luck, on television, in front of the world.

五

People bid for it

Phone numbers, license plates and lucky addresses full of eights sell at auction for sums that make no sense until you understand the number.

六

Buildings are numbered around it

Developers price the 8th and 88th floors higher and quietly skip the 4th, because four sounds like the word for death. Luck is literally built into the elevator panel.

七

Eight is a whole system

The bāguà, the eight trigrams, map the whole world in feng shui and the I Ching. Eight already meant completeness before it ever meant money.
